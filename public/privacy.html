Wavo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Wavo is a private social and messaging app for people you choose to connect with. This policy explains what information Wavo uses to provide messaging, friend-scoped sharing, Spaces, plans, polls, activities, notifications, safety features and optional location sharing.

Account and profile information

  • Username and authentication information. The current app signs you in with a username and password. Supabase handles authentication. Wavo does not receive or store your plaintext password.
  • Profile information you choose to add, such as a status, bio, profile image, theme, badge or other profile customisation.
  • Account activity needed to operate features, such as last-active time, message/read state, notification settings, streak or usage counters, and friend or Space membership.

Content you create

Wavo stores content you choose to create or send so it can be delivered to the audience you selected. This can include direct messages, Space messages, Posts, Waves, plans, polls, activities, reactions, nicknames, voice notes, photos, videos and other uploaded chat or Wave media.

Location, microphone, media and notifications

  • Location: Wavo requests location only when you choose a location or arrival-sharing feature. You can choose approximate or precise sharing where available, and active arrival shares are designed to expire rather than run continuously.
  • Microphone: microphone access is requested when you choose to create a voice note.
  • Photos and videos: media is accessed only when you choose a file or media item to share.
  • Notifications: if you enable notifications, Wavo stores the device/push subscription information needed to deliver them. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

Safety and support

If you block or report someone, or contact Wavo support, we store the information needed to apply the block, review the report, respond to the request and protect the service. Reports may include the user or content involved and the reason you provide.

How we use information

  • To authenticate accounts and operate Wavo features.
  • To deliver content only to the people or Spaces allowed to receive it.
  • To provide optional notifications, location sharing and personalisation.
  • To detect abuse, review reports, enforce blocks and moderate content.
  • To diagnose problems, keep the service reliable and answer support requests.

Wavo does not sell personal information and does not show third-party advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, realtime and file-storage services.
  • Vercel hosts Wavo's web services and public support/legal pages.
  • GIPHY may receive a GIF search when a Wavo feature uses GIF search.
  • Stripe processes payments made through Wavo's website. The native iOS app does not collect card details.

These providers process information needed to provide their part of the service under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your controls

  • Audience controls: Posts and other sharing features let you limit content to friends, selected people or a Space, depending on the feature.
  • Privacy controls: the You tab includes controls such as online status, read receipts, friend requests and default location-sharing precision.
  • Block and report: use the in-app Safety button to report a person, block or unblock someone, review Community Standards or contact support.
  • Delete your account: while signed in, open Safety → Delete account. After confirmation, Wavo deletes the account and associated Wavo data handled by the deletion process. This cannot be undone.

Retention

We keep account information and user content while it is needed to provide Wavo or until it is deleted. Temporary features can have shorter expiry periods. When you delete your account, the live account and associated data handled by the deletion process are removed. Limited copies can remain for a short period in provider backups until those backups rotate out.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, account-deletion help or a parent/guardian enquiry, contact contact@builtbyjake.site or use the in-app Safety Centre while signed in.

Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, the date above will be updated.

Wavo aims to collect only the information needed to operate the features you choose to use.
