Wavo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Wavo is a social messaging app. This policy explains what information Wavo processes, why it is needed, which services help us run the app, and the controls available to you.

Who we are

Wavo is an independent project. If you have a question about privacy or your data, contact us at contact@builtbyjake.site.

What we collect

  • Account information, including your chosen username and the authentication information needed to sign you in. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; Wavo does not receive your password as readable plain text.
  • Profile information you choose to add, such as a profile photo, bio or status.
  • Content and social activity, such as messages, group or Space content, posts, Waves, plans, polls, reactions, nicknames, friendships and files or media you upload.
  • App and device information needed for features, such as last-active information, feature usage records and push-notification device tokens.
  • Location information only when you choose a location-sharing feature. Wavo does not need continuous location access just to use messaging.
  • Purchase and entitlement information used by Wavo's web service when paid Wavo features are purchased or managed there. The submitted iOS app is free-only and does not activate paid Wavo digital features.
  • Connected-service information when you choose to connect an optional service, such as Spotify.
  • Reports and moderation records when content or an account is reported, so safety issues can be reviewed.

How we use it

  • To create and secure accounts and keep you signed in.
  • To deliver messages and media and provide Spaces, posts, Waves, plans, polls, calls, notifications and other Wavo features.
  • To provide features you explicitly enable, including location sharing and connected services.
  • To operate, troubleshoot and improve Wavo.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate reports and enforce Wavo's rules.

We do not sell your personal information, and Wavo does not show third-party advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, realtime and file-storage services used by Wavo.
  • Vercel hosts Wavo's web services.
  • Apple provides iOS platform services such as push notifications.
  • Stripe may process purchases made through Wavo's website. Wavo does not receive your full card details.
  • GIPHY provides GIF search when that feature is used; the search request is sent to GIPHY.
  • Spotify processes Spotify account information when you choose to connect Spotify to Wavo.

When Wavo shares information with a service provider to operate the app, we require that provider to handle the information with protections that are at least equivalent to the protections described in this policy and required by applicable platform rules and law. Those providers may also publish their own privacy terms for their services.

Your choices and rights

  • Privacy controls: Wavo includes controls for features such as online status, read receipts, friend requests and location sharing.
  • Block and report: you can block people you do not want to interact with and report safety issues.
  • Delete your account: open the You tab, find Account, and choose Delete account. After confirmation, Wavo permanently deletes your account, profile, account-linked social data and Wavo files owned by that account, then signs the account out.
  • Contact us: you can also ask a privacy question using the email address above.

Shared Spaces or other collaborative containers may remain for their other members after you delete your account, but your membership and user-linked content are removed as part of the deletion process.

Data retention

We generally keep account information while your account exists or while it is needed to provide the feature that created it. When you complete in-app account deletion, Wavo removes the active account and associated user data. Limited safety, security, transaction or legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary or required, and records that remain may be detached from the deleted profile where appropriate. Backup copies can also remain temporarily until normal backup retention cycles complete.

Children and families

Wavo may be used by younger users where permitted by the App Store, applicable law and any required parent or guardian permission. If a parent or guardian has a privacy question about a young person's Wavo account, contact contact@builtbyjake.site.

Security

We use access controls and platform security features intended to limit access to Wavo data. No online service can promise absolute security, so we also work to remove or fix issues when they are identified.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. The current version will remain available from Wavo's privacy link.

This policy is written to describe how Wavo currently works, not to make claims about features Wavo does not provide.
