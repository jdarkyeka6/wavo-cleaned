Wavo Support

Last updated: 24 August 2026

For account, privacy, safety or technical help, use the Safety button inside Wavo while signed in. You can also contact us by email.

contact@builtbyjake.site

Include your Wavo username and a short description of what happened. Do not send your password.

Account

Signing in

The current Wavo app signs in with your username and password. If the app says the login is incorrect, check the username spelling and password and try again.

I forgot my password

If you are still signed in on another device, contact support from the in-app Safety Centre. If you are locked out everywhere, email us with your username. Account recovery can require additional verification before any account change is made.

Deleting my account

While signed in, tap the persistent Safety button, choose Delete account, read the warning and complete the confirmation. This permanently deletes the account and associated Wavo data handled by the deletion process. It cannot be undone.

Safety

Blocking someone

Open Safety → Block & unblock, search the username and choose Block. Blocking removes the connection and prevents normal friend/DM access between the accounts. You can return to the same screen to unblock someone.

Reporting someone or content

Open Safety → Report someone, select the person, choose a reason and add details that help moderation identify the content. Waves also provide a report option from their action menu. Reports go to Wavo moderation for review.

Community Standards

The Safety Centre includes Wavo's Community Standards. Harassment, credible threats, hate or discriminatory abuse, sexual exploitation, scams, impersonation, privacy abuse and repeated unwanted contact are not allowed.

Privacy and location

The You tab includes privacy controls for online status, read receipts, friend requests and default location-sharing precision. Wavo asks for location only when you choose a location or arrival-sharing action. Active shares can be stopped from the You tab.

Notifications on iPhone

The native iOS app can request notification permission when you choose Enable notifications. If permission was denied, open iOS Settings → Notifications → Wavo to change it. Notifications are optional.

Voice notes and media

Microphone access is used only when you choose to record a voice note. Photos or videos are accessed when you choose media to share. If a permission is off, you can change it in iOS Settings for Wavo.

Premium

The native iOS app does not present external checkout or Stripe purchase controls. If an account already has Premium entitlements, Wavo can display the associated account benefits. For a billing issue with a transaction made outside the iOS app, contact support.

Bugs

Tell us what screen you were on, what you tapped, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot is useful when it does not expose private information.
