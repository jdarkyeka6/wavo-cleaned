# App Store screenshot capture plan

Use the actual Release candidate on a physical or simulator iPhone with a populated review/demo account. Do not use mock screens that show features the submitted binary does not contain.

Recommended screenshot sequence:

1. **Home** — show Posts/Waves and the visibly labelled Safety button.
   Caption idea: “Your people, without the noise.”
2. **Inbox / DM** — show a real conversation and mobile back navigation.
   Caption idea: “Messages that stay useful.”
3. **Space** — show a Space with chat plus a plan/poll/activity context.
   Caption idea: “One place for the whole group.”
4. **Create** — show the creation menu with Post, Wave, Plan, Poll, Activity and Space.
   Caption idea: “Turn chat into something happening.”
5. **Private audience** — show selected-person sharing for a Post/Wave.
   Caption idea: “Choose exactly who sees it.”
6. **Plan or Poll** — show RSVPs or voting.
   Caption idea: “Decide and organise together.”
7. **Safety Centre** — show Report, Block, Community Standards, Contact Wavo and Delete account.
   Caption idea: “Built-in controls when you need them.”

Before capture:

- remove test profanity or placeholder content
- use realistic but non-sensitive demo conversations
- make sure usernames/avatars are okay to publish on the App Store
- do not show passwords, email addresses, precise location coordinates or private real-world messages
- keep the iPhone status bar clean and consistent across captures
