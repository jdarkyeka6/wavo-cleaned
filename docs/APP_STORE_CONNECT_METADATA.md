## Description
Wavo keeps conversations and real plans together.

Message the people you know, create private Spaces for groups, share Posts and quick Waves with friends or selected people, vote in polls, organise plans and activities, and keep up without an open public follower network.

Highlights:
- Direct messages and group Spaces
- Friend-scoped Posts and Waves
- Selected-person sharing controls
- Plans, RSVPs, polls and activities
- Reactions and voice notes
- Offline-friendly messaging
- Optional arrival/location sharing
- Native notifications
- In-app reporting, blocking and account deletion

Wavo is designed around people you choose to connect with. Privacy controls let you manage presence, read receipts, friend requests and location-sharing precision.
